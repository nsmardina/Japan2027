Coeliac card

A Japanese explanation of your dietary requirement.

JAPANESE
私はセリアック病です。小麦、大麦、ライ麦を食べることができません。
Watashi wa seriakku-byō desu. Komugi, ōmugi, raimugi o taberu koto ga dekimasen.
ENGLISH MEANINGI have coeliac disease. I cannot eat wheat, barley or rye.

Please also explain the risk of cross-contamination and ask staff to confirm whether they can safely accommodate this requirement.
